02 / RECEIVE

Hear the far end

Choose a receiver near the station you want to reach. Tune to the agreed dial and mode, then listen for an identifiable call.

Worldwide SDR receiver map ↗KiwiSDR public receivers ↗

A change in the noise floor alone does not confirm your signal. Receiver availability and antenna quality vary.

03 / ANTENNAS

Half-wave inverted V

Starting wire length using 142.65 ÷ MHz (metres). Insulated ends; apex height assumes ends at ground level. Actual resonance depends on height, ground and wire. Measure and trim; this is not an earth-connected antenna model.

04 / EXPORT

Take the map with you

Search or filter the station map, then export that set.

KML contains static point overlays. SitaWare support depends on your version and import tools; GeoJSON is an interchange file, not a native SitaWare package. No live TAK feed is provided.

05 / DATA CHECKS

Agree both ends first

Record the modem and waveform, baud rate, ALE generation, time synchronisation and protection settings in the coordination notes. Confirm these directly with the other operator.

Log data or AMD separately from voice. Use the issued instructions for the current serial and scoring.

Yours to keep

The shared roster is a dated registration snapshot. Search and map planning happen in your browser; contact logs, imported channels and station settings are saved on this device.

Back up before changing devices or clearing browser data. Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap and space weather from NOAA.
